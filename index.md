### Why Keep a Process Journal?

A process journal serves several key purposes:

1. **Memory aid** — Records exact parameters, settings, materials, and sources so I don't have to repeat work or research.
2. **Idea development** — A place to sketch out concepts, list possibilities, and track thought processes.
3. **Legal protection** — Contemporaneous notes can serve as evidence of original work and due diligence.

### Key Principles

- Record everything — more than you think you need
- Don't over-format — just get it down
- Make the workflow as frictionless as possible
